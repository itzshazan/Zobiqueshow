06 / FAQ

Questions, answered.

What's the difference between Zobique and Zobique Labs?

Zobique is a career intelligence platform for students, graduates and universities. Zobique Labs is our engineering studio, building custom AI agents, copilots and automation for businesses.

What makes the Zobique platform different from other job sites?

It doesn't just list jobs. It predicts the roles you fit, optimizes your resume for each application, prepares you for interviews and tracks everything in one place.

How fast can Labs get an AI system into production?

Typically between 2 and 16 weeks, depending on scope, through a Discover, Design, Build and Launch process.

Is my company's data safe with Labs?

Systems are built with strict data privacy, SOC2 compliance standards and role-based access controls, including private deployments with zero data leakage.

Does the platform cover careers outside computer science?

Yes. Career guidance, certifications and course recommendations extend beyond CS, and job suggestions take your location into account.
